The length check and enqueue must be one atomic operation under a lock or an atomic reservation. Individual deque operations do not protect this compound invariant. Prefer a standard bounded queue and a separate in-flight budget, both accounting for bytes as well as item counts.
On saturation, wait only until timeout and then reject. Release reservations on failure or cancel; close/shutdown wakes blocked producers and consumers. Keep expensive work outside the lock. Test a controlled interleaving of two producers racing for the final slot, consumption during shutdown, cancellation and duplicate completion. Verify capacity, ownership and absence of deadlock before throughput testing.
