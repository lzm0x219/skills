Accept a caller-provided allocator and return a result whose allocated fields are all owned by the caller. Provide `deinit(allocator)` for the result and state that borrowed parser views must not outlive the backing buffer.

Use `defer` for the file and temporary buffer, and protect every allocation being transferred into the result with `errdefer` until construction succeeds. Preserve I/O and parsing failures through an explicit error union (`!Result`) rather than `catch unreachable`. Test success, malformed input, allocation failure when supported by the environment, and cleanup with a leak-detecting test allocator.
