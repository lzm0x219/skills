Choose between `@cImport` and `zig translate-c` based on the header, macro, and cflags requirements, then confine generated bindings to a narrow adapter. Verify the target, calling convention, integer widths, alignment, `extern` layout, and link model; never infer another target's ABI from the local layout.

A buffer returned by C must be released by the C library's designated function, not a Zig allocator. This ownership boundary is part of the adapter contract. Callback userdata needs a stable owner, must remain valid from registration through unregistration, and must have explicit thread, reentrancy, and unregistration-order rules. Verify the boundary by compiling the binding, linking a real or test library, running successful and failing calls in a compatible environment, and registering, invoking, and unregistering the cross-thread callback.
